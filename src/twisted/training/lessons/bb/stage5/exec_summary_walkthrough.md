## What We Are Doing

The executive summary must communicate without technical jargon. Use this template as a guide:

Opening paragraph: "Between [start date] and [end date], we conducted a security assessment of [client name]'s [scope description]. We identified [X] critical, [Y] high, [Z] medium, and [W] low-severity vulnerabilities."

Key findings paragraph: Name the top three most serious findings in plain language. "The most severe issue allows an attacker to access the entire customer database without providing any login credentials. A second issue allows anyone to access the administrator control panel. A third issue allows attackers to send fraudulent emails appearing to come from [client]."

Business impact paragraph: "These vulnerabilities create a direct risk of data breach affecting [number] customers, potential regulatory penalties under [applicable regulation], and reputational damage if exploited."

Immediate actions paragraph: "We recommend three immediate actions: [action 1], [action 2], [action 3]. These can be implemented within 48-72 hours and significantly reduce immediate risk while permanent fixes are developed."

Positive observations (if any): "Several security controls were found to be well-implemented, including [examples]."
